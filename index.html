Texture reCreator Studio — Documentation & FAQ

Overview & 4-Way Mirror Seamless Algorithm

Texture reCreator Studio is a free, in-browser tool engineered for 3D artists, game developers, architectural visualizers, and UI designers to test, generate, and preview seamless tileable textures in real time.

Standard grid tiling creates obvious, distracting grid lines because opposite image boundaries rarely match. The 4-Way Mirror Algorithm reverses adjacent image tiles horizontally and vertically, guaranteeing that touching boundary edges share identical pixel color and gradient values. The result is a mathematically seamless texture without manual stamp or clone-tool retouching in photo software.

Interactive 3D PBR Inspection Rigs

Preview how textures react to curvature, contact lighting, and material relief across 6 dedicated inspection environments powered by Three.js and soft PCF contact shadows:

  • 3D Sphere: Evaluates specular highlights, Fresnel reflectivity, and continuous radial curvature.
  • 3D Cube: Tests hard-surface 90° edge continuity and corner seam alignment.
  • 3D Column: Validates cylindrical wrapping for architectural pillars, pipes, and trunks.
  • 3D Drape: Inspects soft organic fabric folds, cloth textiles, curtains, and banners.
  • 150mm Architectural Room: An accurate L-shaped double interior corner wall (150mm thickness, chamfered ends, neutral floor) to preview wallpaper, plaster, tiles, and concrete.
  • 2D Seamless Wall: A full planar view simulating a seamless flat wall surface.

Photoshop-Style Proportional Scaling

Control texture frequency with Photoshop-accurate aspect-ratio locking:

  • Linked (Proportional): Displays a single Tile Scale slider. Both width and height scale harmoniously in tandem, preserving natural pixel aspect ratios.
  • Unlinked (Independent): Splits into independent Width and Height sliders for custom anisotropic compression or stretching.

Workflow Integration: Blender, Unreal Engine 5, Unity & Figma

Once your tile is dialed in:

  • Download Texture: Saves the processed high-resolution JPEG directly into your downloads folder.
  • Copy to Clipboard: Direct raster copy ready to paste immediately into Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator, or Canva.
  • 3D Snap: Takes a pristine, high-resolution viewport render of your chosen 3D model on the studio cyclorama.
  • Blender / Unreal Import: Drop the downloaded texture directly into your Principled BSDF or Material Graph as your Base Color (Albedo) map.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Texture reCreator 100% free and private?

Yes. Texture reCreator runs 100% client-side inside your browser via HTML5 Canvas and WebGL. Your uploaded textures, photos, and files are never sent to or stored on any remote server.

How do I create a seamless texture online for free?

Drag and drop any image into the workspace, select 4-Way Mirror tiling pattern, and adjust the scale slider until satisfied. Click Download Texture to save the seamless result.

What is the difference between standard tiling and 4-way mirror tiling?

Standard tiling repeats the texture in a direct grid, creating hard visible seams if the source image is not pre-seamless. 4-Way Mirror inverts alternating rows and columns so edge borders blend seamlessly with zero visible seam artifacts.

How do I inspect bump and normal depth?

Adjust the Surface Relief (Bump) slider in the sidebar. The WebGL shader computes procedural luminance gradients in real time, simulating surface height and tactile roughness.
